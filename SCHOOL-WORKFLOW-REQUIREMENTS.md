# School administration requirements — pending inventory and implementation

- Administration creates grades, classes and timetables.
- Educational affairs deputy registers teachers and specialties and assigns classes.
- Student affairs deputy registers students and guardians and assigns students to classes.
- Administration assigns supervisors the teachers they monitor and evaluate.
- General supervisor determines permissions.

Inspect existing identities, roles, schedules and access boundaries first. Extend existing systems and test denied cross-class access. This page/audio update does not implement these requirements.

Scheduling must prevent assigning a teacher to concurrent classes. A necessary exception may allow multiple classes only when stage, subject and lesson topic all match. This is a requirement, not an implemented scheduling rule.
