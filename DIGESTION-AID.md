# Digestion teaching aid — local draft
Original short Arabic sequence for a proposed grade-six science lesson; not a copied textbook diagram or an assertion of current curriculum alignment.
Medical fact reference reviewed: https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/digestive-system-how-it-works
Educational lead provided: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dNWznqU_TG4 (Ain lesson; direct retrieval throttled during this review, current textbook edition not verified).
Route includes mouth, pharynx, esophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine, rectum, anus. Distinguishes accessory organs and fecal elimination from kidney excretion.
